# Welcome to Casa Oliva

_Internal guest guide — revised for the 2026 season_

We are happy to welcome you to **Casa Oliva**, our restored stone cottage just outside
Lucca. The house is in a quiet residential lane rather than in the historic centre, so a
car is useful but not essential. The official address used for deliveries is:

> Via delle Ginestre 18, 55100 Lucca LU, Italy

Please do not direct large vans through the small arch beside the church; the turn is too
tight. The easier approach is from Via della Chiesa.

## Before arrival

The home is prepared for **up to six registered guests**. Sleeping arrangements are spread
across three rooms: one room with a queen bed, one with two single beds, and the small garden
room with a double sofa bed. The garden room has curtains but gets bright early in summer.

Our local team normally finishes preparing the house by mid-afternoon. Guests may arrive
from **4:00 p.m. onward**. If the house is ready earlier, the host will confirm this in the
booking conversation; please do not promise early entry in advance.

> **Old printed booklet:** Some copies in the utility drawer say check-in begins at 3:00 p.m.
> This is no longer correct. The current arrival time is 4:00 p.m.

On departure day, please leave by **10:30 a.m.** so the cleaning team has enough time before
the next stay.

## Finding and entering the house

Casa Oliva is the second stone house after the green post box. The name is painted on a
small ceramic tile beside the wooden gate; it can be difficult to see after dark.

Parking on the lane blocks the neighbours. Instead, continue about 40 metres beyond the
house and use the gravel area on the left. There is space for two normal-sized cars. Keep
the marked centre strip clear because it is shared with number 20.

The pedestrian entrance is through the wooden garden gate. The arrival message sent on the
morning of check-in explains where the key is collected. Do not include door, alarm, key-safe,
or access codes in public guidebooks or automated replies.

The kitchen has an induction hob, oven, dishwasher, moka pot, kettle, and a full-size fridge
with a small freezer compartment. Olive oil, salt, tea, and a starter supply of coffee are
provided. The outdoor dining table seats six. There is also a travel cot in the wardrobe,
although guests should bring their own cot linen.

### A few house expectations

- The property is non-smoking indoors, including at open windows.
- Quiet hours begin at **10:00 p.m.**; voices carry easily between the stone houses.
- No parties, events, or unregistered overnight visitors.
- Dogs are considered only when approved before arrival. Cats are not accepted.
- Please use plastic cups, not glass, around the small plunge pool.
- The fireplace is decorative and must not be lit.
- Before departure, place tied rubbish bags in the bins by the gravel parking area.

The plunge pool is generally available from May through September, depending on weather.
It is not heated and has no lifeguard. Children must be supervised at all times.

## Useful places nearby

For breakfast, **Forno del Borgo** is about an eight-minute walk toward the church and is
usually closed on Mondays. **Trattoria da Lina**, roughly 1.5 km away, is popular for dinner;
booking ahead is sensible in July and August. The nearest small grocery shop is Alimentari
Rossi on Via Pesciatina. Larger supermarkets are closer to Lucca.

The number 6 bus stops near the church, but evening service is limited. Lucca's walls are
approximately fifteen minutes away by car when traffic is light.

## Maintenance notes that guests may see

The left-hand bedroom shutter sticks in damp weather. Lift it slightly before pulling it
closed. The dishwasher's eco cycle is marked with a leaf symbol and takes longer than the
other programmes; this is normal.

If something is damaged or not working, guests should contact the host through the booking
channel so the correct local team can respond. General Italian emergency services can be
reached on 112.
